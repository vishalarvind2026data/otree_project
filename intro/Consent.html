Academic Research Study: Urban Commute Preferences

Welcome to this scientific research study conducted to examine commuter decision-making, travel mode choices, and transport policies.

Study Overview
  • Procedure: You will review 8 distinct commuting scenarios describing hypothetical work journeys with realistic travel times, weather conditions, and costs. In each scenario, you will choose your preferred mode of transportation. Afterward, you will complete a brief survey on your travel habits, preferences, and background.
  • Duration: The study takes approximately 10 to 12 minutes to complete.
  • Confidentiality: Your responses will be anonymized and used strictly for academic research and statistical analysis. No personally identifying information is published or shared.
  • Voluntary Participation: Participation is completely voluntary. You are free to withdraw at any time.
