How the Commute Scenarios Work

In the upcoming choice task, you will face 8 separate commuting situations representing daily journeys between your residence and your workplace.

Journey Context

Each situation describes the commute distance (8 to 35 km) and current weather conditions (e.g. Sunny, Rain, Cloudy).

Itemised Costs

Each option displays itemised monthly costs (fuel, parking fees, public transit pass fares) along with estimated per-trip equivalents based on 22 working days per month.

Travel Times

Each option shows the one-way door-to-door travel time in minutes under typical rush-hour traffic conditions.

Your Task: In each of the 8 scenarios, please review the options carefully and select the mode of travel you would genuinely choose for your daily commute.
