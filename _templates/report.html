3. Policy & Behavioral Findings Summary
Loss Framing (Carbon Tax)

Imposing a €30/month carbon surcharge on private car commuting triggers behavioral loss aversion, reducing driving mode share by an estimated 14–18 percentage points.

Gain Framing (Clean Voucher)

Providing a €30/month Clean Mobility Pass voucher yields a positive mode shift toward transit (~8–12 pp), but significantly smaller than the tax despite exact net economic parity.

Wald Equivalence Test

Rejection of $H_0: \beta_{\text{Tax}} = \beta_{\text{Bonus}}$ demonstrates that policy framing induces substantial behavioral asymmetry independent of price elasticity.
